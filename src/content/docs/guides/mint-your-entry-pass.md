title: Mint Your Entry Pass
---

In Conquest.eth "Faction Mod", the tokenomics are very different from previous iteration. Instead of player getting tokens by coverting $ to token, they have to join a faction that then give them token over time. And note that these tokens are not backed by $.

So, to make sure player cannot just have access to infinite token, each player that want to join a faction needs to first get access to an "Entry Pass" NFT.

To do, so just navigate to [Conquest's Entry Pass website](https://entry-pass.conquest.game) and mint your pass.

:::note
This pass has another purpose. This version of Conquest.eth runs on the [Endurance network](https://ace.fusionist.io/) which has its own Native token (ACE) which is not easy to get depending on your country of residence.

To solve, this minting the Entry Pass will also give you enough ACE to play the game.
:::


## Entry Pass is minted on Base

To make it easy for you to mint the Entry Pass, we made it available on Base. 

As mentioned just navigate to [Conquest's Entry Pass website](https://entry-pass.conquest.game) and mint your to play!

Once you got it, you ll be able to choose the faction of your choice.
