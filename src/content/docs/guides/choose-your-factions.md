title: Choose Your Faction
---


Once you got your [Entry Pass](/guides/entry-pass), the game will asl you to choose your faction.

This is an irreversible action and will assign you this faction until the end of the game (June 18th).

## What does faction do ?

In traditional Conquest.eth, players were sole governor and they could dynamically creates alliances with other player to get stronger.

In "Faction Mod", players are assigned a faction that dictate which players are their enemy and which are their friends.

## Token distribution

Furthermore, factions are also how players get tokens to play. Every day tokens are distributed to each factions and each players get a share of these token that they can use to stake on planets.

Note though, that any unused tokens are discarded each day. So make sure you use them as much as you can.
